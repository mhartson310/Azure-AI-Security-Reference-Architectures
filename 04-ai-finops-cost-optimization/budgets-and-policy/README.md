# Budget controls and governance

Azure Cost Management budget alerts **do not automatically stop spend**. Billing telemetry can lag.

Apply cost attribution by workload/team/environment; budget thresholds and owner notifications; supported policy restrictions on resources/regions; quotas or automation where appropriate; approval for costly changes; tested idle shutdown of dev resources; and monthly actual-versus-forecast reviews.

**Validate:** notifications reach intended owners, unauthorized budget edits are denied, policy blocks supported disallowed deployments, cost attribution works, and resource shutdown actually changes billed consumption.
